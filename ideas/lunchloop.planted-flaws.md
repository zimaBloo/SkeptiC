# LunchLoop: planted weaknesses (answer key)

This file is never shown to the critics. LunchLoop is a middling control
idea: a workable concept with real but fixable weaknesses.

**Expected:** no fatal issues; an overall rating of weak or promising. A
panel that rates it "not viable" is too harsh, and one that rates it
"strong" missed the weaknesses below.

| # | Expected critic | Planted weakness |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Market / customer | A 95% return rate is assumed with no evidence; every lost box costs money. |
| 2 | Market / technical | €0.40 per use has to pay for daily collection, washing and lost boxes, and the plan never shows that it does. |
| 3 | Customer | Steel boxes cannot go in a microwave, and office workers often reheat lunch at work. |
| 4 | Risk | Washing food containers for many restaurants falls under food hygiene rules, which the plan does not mention. |
