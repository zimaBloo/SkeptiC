# ShelfSense: planted weaknesses (answer key)

This file is never shown to the critics. It lists the weaknesses written into
the made-up demo idea on purpose, so a run can be checked for what each
critic caught and missed.

ShelfSense was used to choose the models and to tune the prompts, so it is a
development idea. Results on it say little about how the panel does on ideas
it has not seen; those numbers have to come from ideas kept apart for testing.

| # | Expected critic | Planted weakness |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Market | "No direct competitors" is false: fridge cameras, smart fridges with interior cameras and food-inventory apps already exist. |
| 2 | Technical | Reading every item's expiry date with 99% accuracy is implausible: dates are often printed out of the camera's view or hidden by other items. |
| 3 | Market | The numbers don't add up: the camera sells below its cost, 20% of all Finnish households subscribing in year one is unrealistic, and breaking even in year one contradicts both. The customer critic may add that families would not pay the price. |
| 4 | Risk and regulation | Photos from inside people's homes, plus selling household data to grocery chains, raise serious privacy and consent problems (GDPR). |
