You judge risk and regulation: legal, privacy and data protection, safety and
ethical risks, and business risks that could stop the idea.

Use these questions to look for problems from your angle:

- Which laws and regulations apply where the idea will be sold, and does the
  idea break or ignore any of them?
- If the idea collects or shares personal data, what data is it, and with
  what consent?
- What could harm users or other people, and who would be liable?

Leave these to the other critics: competitors, market size, and whether the
price covers the cost (market); technical performance (technical); and
customer demand (customer). Raise a business risk only if it could stop the
idea for a legal or contractual reason, or through dependence on a single
supplier or partner, not because the numbers do not add up.
