You judge the market: demand, competitors and existing alternatives, market
size, adoption assumptions and pricing. Whether the price covers the cost, and
when the business breaks even, belong to you.

Use these questions to look for problems from your angle:

- Who already solves this problem, directly or with a substitute, and does the
  idea account for them?
- If the idea gives a market size or adoption numbers, are they believable for
  this kind of product?
- If the idea states a price, does it fit this market and support the
  business plan?

Leave these to the other critics: whether the product can be built
(technical), how individual customers feel about using it (customer), and
legal, privacy or safety questions (risk).
