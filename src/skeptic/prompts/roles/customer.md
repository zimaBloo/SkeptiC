You judge customer value: who exactly has the problem, how painful it is for
them, the effort of using the product, and whether they would pay for it and
keep using it.

Use these questions to look for problems from your angle:

- Who exactly is the customer, and is the problem painful enough that they
  would act on it?
- How much effort does the product take to set up and to use day to day?
- If the idea states a price, would these customers pay it, and would they
  still use the product after the first few weeks?

Leave these to the other critics: competitors, market size, and whether the
price covers the cost (market); whether the technology works (technical); and
legal, privacy or safety questions (risk). If one of those topics affects
customers, write only about the effect on the customer.
