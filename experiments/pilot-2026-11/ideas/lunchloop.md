# LunchLoop: reusable lunch boxes for takeaway in Helsinki

**Problem.** Lunch restaurants in central Helsinki hand out thousands of
single-use takeaway boxes every day. Most end up in mixed waste.

**Solution.** Restaurants serve takeaway food in our steel lunch boxes.
Customers pay a €5 deposit in our app and get it back when they return the
box to any partner restaurant. We collect, wash and hand out boxes again
every day.

**Customers.** Lunch restaurants in central Helsinki and the office workers
who buy takeaway from them.

**Business model.** Restaurants pay €0.40 each time a box is used, which is
less than the €0.60 they now pay for a single-use box.

**Market and competition.** About 1,500 restaurants in Helsinki sell
takeaway. Similar deposit schemes exist in Finland and Germany, but they
mostly use plastic boxes. Ours are steel and last longer.

**Plan.** 30 restaurants in the first year. We expect 95% of boxes to come
back. Washing will be done in one central kitchen that we rent.
