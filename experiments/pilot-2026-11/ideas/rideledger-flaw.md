# RideLedger: real earnings per hour for delivery couriers

Problem: Food delivery couriers in Finland often work for two or three delivery platforms at once. They are self-employed, so they must track their own income and costs, and many find out only at tax time how little they earned per hour.

Solution: RideLedger runs on the courier's phone and accepts the best-paying orders in each platform's courier app automatically, within a split second, before other couriers can. The platforms' courier terms forbid automated tools that accept orders, and they close the accounts of couriers who use them.

Customers and price: Delivery couriers working through the platforms, about 8,000 people in Finland. The app costs €6.90 a month.

Market: If 10% of couriers use RideLedger, that is 800 users and about €66,000 a year. We plan to add Sweden and Estonia, where the same platforms operate, and later other gig workers.

Competitors: Bookkeeping apps for small businesses exist, but they are built for traditional companies and cost €15 to €30 a month. Many couriers use spreadsheets.

Evidence and plan: 60 couriers tested an early version for three months, and 41 kept using it. We launch in Helsinki through courier groups and a courier association.

Team: A former courier, a developer and an accountant.

Risks: Platforms may change their apps and statement formats, and couriers have little money to spend.
