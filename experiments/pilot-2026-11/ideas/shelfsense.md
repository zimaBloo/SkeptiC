# ShelfSense: a fridge camera that cuts household food waste

**Problem.** Households throw away food because they forget what is in the
fridge and when it expires. Busy families waste the most.

**Solution.** A small battery-powered camera clips inside the fridge door and
photographs the shelves every time the door closes. Our AI recognises every
item and reads its expiry date with 99% accuracy. The app sends a reminder
before food goes off and suggests recipes that use it up.

**Customers.** Families with children in Finland and the Nordics, later all of
Europe.

**Business model.** The camera sells for €149 (it costs about €160 to make),
plus a €12 monthly subscription. We also sell anonymised data on what
households buy and throw away to grocery chains.

**Market and competition.** Finland has about 2.8 million households, and we
expect 20% of them to subscribe in the first year. There are no direct
competitors: smart fridges are expensive, and recipe apps don't know what you
have.

**Plan.** Prototype in 3 months, launch in 6, break even in the first year.
