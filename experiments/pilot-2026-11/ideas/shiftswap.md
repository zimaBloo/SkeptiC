# ShiftSwap: shift trading for hospital nurses

Problem: Nurses in Finnish hospitals often need to swap shifts because of illness in the family, studies or childcare. Today a swap means messages in WhatsApp groups, phone calls and a form that the ward manager approves by hand. A swap takes on average two days to arrange, and ward managers spend up to five hours a week on it.

Solution: ShiftSwap is a mobile app where a nurse offers a shift and colleagues with the right qualifications can take it with one tap. Our AI matches swaps in seconds and checks automatically that every swap follows the Working Hours Act and the collective agreement, so the ward manager only confirms. There are no direct competitors: no one offers shift trading built for nurses.

Customers: The wellbeing services counties, which employ most of Finland's 75,000 nurses. Nurses use the app for free; the county pays.

Business model: €4 per nurse per month. With all counties on board, that is €3.6 million a year.

Market and plan: We will build the app in three months and start a pilot in one county within six months. In year two we expect half of all wellbeing services counties to use ShiftSwap, and we plan to expand to Sweden and Norway in year three.

Team: Two software developers and one former nurse.
