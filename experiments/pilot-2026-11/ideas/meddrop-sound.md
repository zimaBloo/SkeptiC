# MedDrop: same-day delivery of pharmacy orders in Helsinki

Problem: People who are ill, recovering from surgery or caring for small children often cannot get to a pharmacy. Pharmacy home delivery in Helsinki usually takes one to three days.

Solution: MedDrop is a delivery service for licensed pharmacies. The customer orders through the pharmacy's own web shop as today, the pharmacist checks and packs the order, and our courier collects the sealed bag and delivers it within three hours, checking the recipient's identity on delivery.

Customers and price: Pharmacies pay €5.50 per delivery and decide what to charge their customers. Our courier cost is about €3.80 per delivery, with eight deliveries an hour per courier.

Market: Helsinki has about 80 pharmacies. If 30 of them send 20 deliveries a day each, that is about 600 deliveries a day and about €1.2 million a year.

Competitors: General food and parcel couriers already deliver for some pharmacies, but without identity checks or temperature-controlled bags. Large pharmacy chains run their own delivery in some cities.

Plan: Three pharmacies have signed letters of intent for a six-month pilot from March; after that we add pharmacies in pairs. Team: a logistics manager, a developer and a pharmacist adviser.

Risks: Rules on handing medicines to the right person, keeping cold medicines cold, and pharmacies choosing a cheaper general courier.
