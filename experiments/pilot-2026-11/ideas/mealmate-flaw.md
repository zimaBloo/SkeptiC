# MealMate: hot meals delivered to older people at home in Tampere

Problem: Many older people in Tampere live alone and find it hard to cook every day. Relatives worry, and the city's own meal service has a waiting list of about four weeks.

Solution: MealMate cooks hot, nutritionally balanced lunches in a rented central kitchen and delivers them every weekday on fixed routes. The driver checks that the customer is well and sends relatives a short message if something seems wrong.

Customers and price: Older people living alone, and their relatives, who usually order for them. Customers pay with the city's meal service voucher, which covers at most €6.50 per meal; they pay nothing themselves and cannot add money to it. Ingredients and cooking cost us €4.20 per meal and a delivery on a route of 25 stops costs €2.80.

Market: Tampere has about 18,000 people over 75 who live alone. We aim for 400 regular customers taking five meals a week by the end of year two, which gives about €680,000 a year in sales.

Competitors: The city's meal service and two private caterers deliver cold meals once or twice a week. We deliver hot meals daily.

Evidence and plan: A three-month trial with 35 customers kept 30 of them. We will grow route by route, adding a van for every 25 customers.

Risks: Food hygiene rules for the kitchen and the vans, and staff costs if routes are not full.
