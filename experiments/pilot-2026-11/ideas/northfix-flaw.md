# NorthFix: mobile repair service for forestry machines in Lapland

Problem: Forestry machines in Lapland work far from repair shops. When a harvester or forwarder breaks down, the machine and its operator can stand still for days while waiting for a mechanic, which costs the contractor about €1,500 a day.

Solution: NorthFix runs fully equipped service vans with experienced mechanics who drive to the machine in the forest. Contractors buy a yearly service contract with planned maintenance and a promise to reach the machine within 24 hours of a breakdown.

Customers and price: Owners of the Kotkamet 200, a harvester model that went out of production in 1998; our mechanics know it better than anyone. A yearly contract costs €4,800 per machine; extra repairs are billed by the hour.

Market: About 40 Kotkamet 200 harvesters are still in use in Finland, and an owner spends on average about €5,000 a year on repairs. We need about €900,000 a year in sales to pay for six mechanics and three vans. Six mechanics with three vans can serve that many machines.

Competitors: Machine makers' dealers have workshops in Rovaniemi and Oulu, and some independent mechanics work alone. None of them promise a 24-hour response across the region.

Evidence and plan: Eight owners with 12 machines have signed letters of intent. We start with two vans in the Rovaniemi area in spring.

Team: Two mechanics with 15 years' experience each and a business manager from the forestry sector.

Risks: Long driving distances in winter, and finding skilled mechanics.
