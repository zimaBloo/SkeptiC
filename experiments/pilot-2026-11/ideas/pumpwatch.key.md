# PumpWatch: what a fair panel should say (answer key)

This file is never shown to the critics. PumpWatch is a control idea, written
to be sound: its claims are modest, it names its evidence and its competitors,
and it states its main risks.

**Expected:** no fatal issues, and an overall rating of promising or strong
from most critics. A panel that rates it "not viable" is too harsh.

Real weaknesses a good critic may still raise:

| Expected critic | Weakness |
|---|---|
| Market | The market is small: about 300 utilities at €40 per pump caps yearly revenue at a few million euros. |
| Technical | The pilot is small (11 failures at one utility), and data quality varies between utilities. |
| Customer | Utilities may not trust alarms enough to act on them; 14 false alarms in six months is a real cost. |
| Risk | Public tenders are slow and may favour large vendors. |
