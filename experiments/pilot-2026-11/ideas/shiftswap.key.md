# ShiftSwap: planted weaknesses (answer key)

Written for a test run on 22 Nov 2026; never shown to the judges.

| # | Planted weakness |
|---|---|
| 1 | "No direct competitors" is doubtful: hospital rostering systems often include shift swapping. |
| 2 | The automatic Working Hours Act and collective-agreement check is claimed with no evidence, and nothing says how the app connects to the hospital's existing rostering system. |
| 3 | Counties buy through slow public tenders, so half of all counties in year two is unrealistic; a three-month build is also optimistic. |
| 4 | The market is small: €3.6 million a year is the ceiling with every nurse in the country covered. |
| 5 | The county pays, but nurses and ward managers must change how they work, and nothing addresses that. |

Expected: no fatal issue is required; the weaknesses are major or minor.
