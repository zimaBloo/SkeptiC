# BalconySun: plug-in solar panels for apartment balconies

Problem: People who live in apartments cannot put solar panels on the roof, but many want to cut their electricity bill and use their own clean power.

Solution: BalconySun sells an 800-watt plug-in solar kit that hangs on a balcony railing and plugs into an ordinary socket. An electrician from our partner network checks the socket and the building's rules before installation. Our app shows how much power the kit has produced.

Customers and price: Apartment owners and tenants in southern Finland with a south-facing balcony. A kit costs €690 installed. Each 800-watt kit produces about 7,000 kWh a year, which at €0.15 per kWh saves about €1,050 a year, and we guarantee it: if a kit produces less than 7,000 kWh in a year, we pay the customer the difference.

Market: About 1.3 million Finnish homes are in apartment buildings, and we estimate that about 10% have a suitable balcony. We aim to sell 3,000 kits in year two, which is about €2 million in sales.

Competitors: Hardware chains sell plug-in kits without installation or a building check. We offer the check, the installation and a two-year warranty.

Evidence and plan: 40 kits have been installed in a pilot in Espoo over the last two months. Team: an electrical engineer, a sales lead and two installers.

Risks: Housing companies may forbid panels on railings, and electricity prices may fall.
